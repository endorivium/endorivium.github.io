Hi, I'm Julia Ihrenberger, and I've been drawing for most of my life but have been seriously working on my skills (mostly digitally) since around 2021.

My inspirations lie in comic books and manga, which put emphasis on lineart and page flow, which I find especially interesting and engaging.

Art Dailies

Within my friend group, art dailies were introduced roughly around 2024 as a way to improve our art/design skills and artistic eye as well as build a consistent habit of drawing daily if possible. They were usually created within a time frame of 0.5 - 1.5hrs per artpiece with a common topic that was decided on beforehand such as existing media or general themes.

This page shows a small selection for each year with 2026 being the most active.
